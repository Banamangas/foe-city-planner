# Download FoE City Planner

FoE City Planner rearranges your Forge of Empires city so it needs fewer
roads. It runs on your own computer: your city never leaves it. There is
nothing else to install.

## Which file do I pick?

Download **one** file, the one for your computer:

| Your computer | File to download |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 | `FoE-City-Planner-Windows.exe` |
| Mac with an Apple chip (M1, M2, M3, M4...) | `FoE-City-Planner-macOS.zip` |
| Linux | `FoE-City-Planner-Linux` |

The app is free and not signed with a paid certificate, so the first time
you open it your computer warns you that it does not know where it comes
from. The steps below get you past that warning once; after that the app
opens normally.

## Windows

1. Double-click `FoE-City-Planner-Windows.exe`.
2. If a blue box says **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**.

   ![The "Windows protected your PC" box, with the More info link](download/windows-1.png)

3. Click **Run anyway**.

   ![The same box after More info, with the Run anyway button](download/windows-2.png)

The app opens in its own window. You can move the file anywhere you like,
for example to your Desktop.

## Mac

1. Double-click `FoE-City-Planner-macOS.zip` to unzip it. You get
   **FoE City Planner**. Drag it into your **Applications** folder if you
   like.
2. The first time, do not double-click it: **right-click** (or Control-click)
   the app and choose **Open**.

   ![Right-click on the app, with Open in the menu](download/macos-1.png)

3. In the box that appears, click **Open** again.

   ![The box asking if you are sure, with the Open button](download/macos-2.png)

If your Mac only offers **Move to Bin** or **Done**, click **Done**, then
open **System Settings** → **Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click
**Open Anyway** next to the line about FoE City Planner.

![Privacy & Security with the Open Anyway button](download/macos-3.png)

## Linux

1. Right-click `FoE-City-Planner-Linux`, choose **Properties**, and turn on
   **Allow executing file as program** (the wording changes a little from one
   system to another).

   ![The file's Properties with the allow executing option turned on](download/linux-1.png)

   Or, if you prefer typing a command: `chmod +x FoE-City-Planner-Linux`

2. Double-click the file. The app opens in your web browser.

## Your data

The app keeps only your last city and where you left off, on your own
computer. Closing the app stops any search that is still running.
